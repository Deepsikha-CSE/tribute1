Mother Teresa

A Symbol of Selfless Love and Service

Who Was She?

Mother Teresa, born in 1910 in Skopje, dedicated her life to helping the poorest of the poor in Kolkata, India. She founded the Missionaries of Charity, a Roman Catholic religious congregation, which continues her work to this day.

Admirable Qualities

  • Compassion: She showed unconditional love to those who were abandoned and suffering.
  • Humility: Despite her global recognition, she lived a simple and humble life.
  • Dedication: She worked tirelessly for the sick, poor, and dying, expecting nothing in return.
  • Faith: Her deep spiritual beliefs gave her strength in challenging times.

Legacy

Mother Teresa received the Nobel Peace Prize in 1979 and was canonized as Saint Teresa of Calcutta in 2016. Her life is a reminder that kindness and service can change the world.
